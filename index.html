M. Atif Zaheer

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Penn State University, working with Amita Malik. My master's thesis supervisor was Emanuel Carneiro.

My research interests lie in analytic number theory, particularly in the theory of Riemann zeta function and L-functions, distribution of primes, and arithmetic statistics.
