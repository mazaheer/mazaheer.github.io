About me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in mathematics at Penn State University, working under Amita Malik. Previously, I completed M.S. in mathematics from Lahore University of Mathematical Sciences, Pakistan. My master's thesis was supervised by Emanuel Carneiro and it was titled "Pair Correlation Conjecture and Bounds for the Averages of Montgomery's F-function".

My research focuses on analytic number theory, particularly pair correlation of zeros of derivatives of completed Dirichlet L-functions, zero-free regions, and moments of L-functions. I have also recently started exploring the field of arithmetic statistics.
