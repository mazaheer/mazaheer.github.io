Mathematics · Penn State

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Penn State University, working with Amita Malik. My research is in analytic number theory, especially the Riemann zeta function, L-functions, and their zeros.

I am interested in pair correlation, the distribution of primes, and arithmetic statistics. My master's thesis, supervised by Emanuel Carneiro, studied Montgomery's pair-correlation conjecture.
